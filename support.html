Tiny Bastion — Support

Contact

Questions, bug reports and ideas: serkanozdemirdev@gmail.com. Please include your phone model and the game's version (Settings → About) so I can help faster.

Where is my progress saved?

Only on your phone. The game never connects to the internet and has no cloud save. Deleting the app or choosing "Reset progress" in the settings removes your progress; it does not move to a new phone.

Purchase and refunds

Reinstalling the game with the same account is free. Refunds are requested from the store you bought it from: Google Play or Apple.

Privacy

The game collects no data: privacy policy.
